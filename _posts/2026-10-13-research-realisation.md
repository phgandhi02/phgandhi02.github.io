I'm making this post so that I can continue to get in the habit of writing something that I will publish daily. I know that it's not as polished or technical as I would like it to be, but with the clock reaching 10:30pm, I figured something is better than nothing.

I wanted to write this post about how I realized that one of the major blockers in my research is the fact that it is so easy to cook up a dream of success and that letting it go is both extremely humbling and freeing. I find that it is part of a greater message of learning to be active and present in the current moment rather than thinking about castles in the sky [^1]. I realized this after talking with a close friend and mentor and decided that I would try something different. I decided that I would try to let go of my expectations for work today and just focus on trying to just make good, simple choices.

I know that everything I've mentioned seems obvious, but as an aspiring researcher, it's a lesson that I'm beginning to learn firsthand. I've got a lot to talk about and explain on this blog and by practicing this habit daily even with posts like this, I hope to publicly make progress in developing useful technical reports that others can make great use of.

Tomorrow I hope to write a post about diffusion models and explain them based on the amazing post by Lillian Weng on [Diffusion Models](https://lilianweng.github.io/posts/2021-07-11-diffusion-models/#prog-distll), Julia Turc's [video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=R0uMcXsfo2o) on Diffusion Models, a tutorial [video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=a4Yfz2FxXiY) by DeepFindr, and some others.
